Privacy Policy

Effective June 7, 2026 iOS app PIPEDA compliant

This privacy policy applies to the mood eats app (hereby referred to as "Application") for mobile devices that was created by Jiwon Park (hereby referred to as "Service Provider") as a Freemium service. This service is intended for use "AS IS".

Information Collection and Use

The Application collects information when you download and use it. This information may include information such as:

  • Your device's Internet Protocol address (e.g. IP address)
  • The pages of the Application that you visit, the time and date of your visit, the time spent on those pages
  • The time spent on the Application
  • The operating system you use on your mobile device

Location Information

To provide local weather information that improves your food recommendations, the Application accesses your approximate location. When you request a recommendation, your approximate coordinates are sent to two third-party providers: Open-Meteo, to retrieve current weather conditions, and BigDataCloud, to convert your coordinates into a city name (a process known as reverse geocoding). Your coordinates themselves are not stored by the Application. The resulting city is recorded together with your account to power features such as personalized insights and your Mood Map. You can delete this data at any time by deleting your account.

The Application uses Artificial Intelligence (AI) technologies to generate personalized food recommendations based on your mood inputs and to generate recipes for recommended dishes. Your mood data and food selections are transmitted to our AI service providers for processing. We do not use your data to train AI models. All AI processing is performed in accordance with this privacy policy and applicable laws. If you have questions about the AI features or data processing, please contact the Service Provider.

The Service Provider may use the information you provided to contact you from time to time to provide you with important information and required notices.

For a better experience, while using the Application, the Service Provider may require you to provide us with certain personally identifiable information, including but not limited to:

Email, name, user ID, mood inputs, food preferences, saved recommendations, city (approximate location), platform tap history, device information, subscription status, transaction identifiers, and purchase history.

The information that the Service Provider requests will be retained by them and used as described in this privacy policy.

Opt-Out Rights

You can stop all collection of information by the Application easily by uninstalling it. You may use the standard uninstall processes as may be available as part of your mobile device or via the mobile application marketplace or network.

You can also delete your account and all associated data directly within the Application's settings, or by contacting the Service Provider.

Data Retention Policy

The Service Provider will retain User Provided data for as long as your account is active. Upon account deletion request, data will be deleted within 30 days, except where retention is required by law (e.g., transaction records, which may be retained for up to 7 years for tax and accounting purposes).

Compliance

This Application complies with the Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA) of Canada.

Children

The Application is intended for users aged 13 and above. The Service Provider does not use the Application to knowingly solicit data from or market to children under the age of 13.

The Service Provider does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13 years of age. In the case the Service Provider discovers that a child under 13 has provided personal information, the Service Provider will immediately delete this from their servers. If you are a parent or guardian and you are aware that your child has provided us with personal information, please contact the Service Provider (privacy@moodeats.io) so that they will be able to take the necessary actions.

Security

The Service Provider is concerned about safeguarding the confidentiality of your information. The Service Provider provides physical, electronic, and procedural safeguards to protect information the Service Provider processes and maintains.
