Frequently asked

What is mood eats?

mood eats helps you decide what to eat based on how you feel. Pick a mood, and it suggests dishes matched to your mood, the weather where you are, and what you're craving. Every pick comes with a recipe to cook at home or a way to find it nearby.

How do the recommendations work?

When you choose a mood, mood eats considers that mood along with your local weather and any cuisine or dietary filters you've set, then suggests five dishes. The more you use the app and rate what you get, the better it learns your taste over time.

Can I set dietary needs or pick a cuisine?

Yes. On the home screen you can add a cuisine you're in the mood for and set your dietary preferences. Your recommendations adjust to fit what you choose.

What does mood eats Pro include?

mood eats is free to start. Pro is an optional subscription that unlocks the full experience. You can manage or cancel your subscription anytime through your Apple ID settings on your device.

Subscriptions are billed through your Apple account, and renew automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period.

How do I manage or cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are handled by Apple. On your iPhone, open Settings, tap your name at the top, then tap Subscriptions to view, change, or cancel mood eats Pro.

How do I delete my account and data?

You can delete your account from within the app, which removes your account and associated data. If you have any trouble, email us and we'll help.

How is my data handled?

We collect only what's needed to make recommendations work, in line with our privacy policy. You can read the full details in our Privacy Policy.
